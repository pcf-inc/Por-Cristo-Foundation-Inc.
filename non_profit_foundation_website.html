Foundation History & Strategic Milestones

2015

Institutional Inception

Established by civic leaders to bridge youth literacy gaps, commencing weekend tutoring operations for 50 primary students.

2018

First Flagship Learning Hub

Constructed and launched our primary community learning center equipped with fiber internet, digital computer labs, and library resources.

2021

Emergency Disaster Response Taskforce

Expanded operational capacity to deploy emergency medical supplies and food logistics across 12 typhoon-vulnerable provinces.

2026

Nationwide Reach Milestone

Surpassed 15,000 verified beneficiaries supported by 3 permanent learning centers and 2 mobile health units.
